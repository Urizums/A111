Business tasks use the prior unchanged materials copied into materials/: expenses.csv grouped by category, exact integer fen, all IDs, JSON+Chinese; package.json extracts explicit actions from notes.txt. The common project plan is copied unchanged into shared/project-plan.json. No completed task output or prior diagnostic is given to ordinary workers. Operators see raw sources/criteria and treatment definitions; these are permitted study conditions, not expected business answers.

The same observer is used in both arms. Record coordinator setup begin immediately before each sample's state/work initialization, setup end after original issue and before actual spawn. Record receive begin before status polling and end after successful original receive. Review begin/end surround independent raw-source review and its evidence. Decision begin/end surround incomplete scaffold generation, final decision construction and its preflight. Commit begin/end surround original commit and final checkpoint/reconcile. Worker stages use shared/Worker_Protocol.md. The first versus last marker is not an invented provider start/end; missing/out-of-order/different-boot markers make that interval unverified. Retain monotonic values and UTC for interpretation.

Per sample caller limits: one business spawn, at most five own-subtree status queries, no interrupt/followup/delegation, at most two local correction attempts per worker or coordinator, at most 80 captured non-marker subprocess calls. These are local study policy, not driver/global enforced resource budgets. Unknown/running remains unknown/running; if those bounds are exhausted preserve the sample and report the blocker. Use at most 45-second native notification waits. Never count waits as native creation/status calls or infer terminal status from silence. Record native intent/args before each call and raw return immediately after, using new files.

Allow equivalent implementation choices for business result wording; source assertions are authoritative. Ordinary worker execution is fresh but operator context is reused across its four samples, with learning/cache/load and parallel-block interference possible. Counterbalancing reduces order imbalance but cannot establish causal generalization from four pairs. Compare worker_reply and coordinator decision separately, and disclose common setup/instrumentation. If treatment policy or required capture fails, retain that pair and mark its efficacy comparison invalid rather than removing it or claiming a gain.
