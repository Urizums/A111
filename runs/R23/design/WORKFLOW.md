# R23 Forge workflow: fresh-food forecast and replenishment plan

Status: executable design plus one bounded author probe. Production, full-horizon results, paper, and independent acceptance remain pending.

## Delivery contract

At the decision time `2026-10-31 18:00 Asia/Shanghai`, produce a frozen plan for `2026-11-01` through `2026-12-12` inclusive: 42 dates × 12 stores × 8 items = 4,032 rows per required table. For each `service_date, store_id, item_id`, deliver a point forecast, a nominal 90% interval, and an integer replenishment quantity. Every daily plan must respect item/store maximum 55, network capacity 1,600 units, and procurement spend 6,000 yuan. The two losses are shortage and waste; procurement is a budget constraint. There is no carryover inventory; unmet demand is lost and leftovers are discarded.

The requested final consumer is a Chinese mathematical modeling paper with traceable computations, plots/tables, interpretation, and limitations. The synthetic offline packet is the only evidence base. Do not search for past solutions or infer future realized demand. The `calendar.holiday` field denotes a fictional business event.

## Source and decision contract

Use only the original requirements and listed packet: `runs/R23/brief/TASK.md`, `runs/R23/brief/INPUTS.json`, R21 raw/reference files and identity-only lock, plus C13 Forge documents and lock. Preserve raw files. Record their hashes in each execution manifest. Do not consume R21 request/problem contents, prior run results/diagnoses, or any R23 acceptance/audit artifact.

The supplied `BASELINE.md` defines the current reference to migrate: `shared_ridge10`, Ridge alpha 10, no intercept, fixed 169-column design (96 store-item intercepts, 56 item-weekday indicators, 8 item time trends in 30-day units, 8 item promotion values, and one global holiday feature scaled by 0.3); nonnegative forecast truncation; unknown promotion filled with the item mean among the preceding 56 training days' announced promotions, then zero. Weather and settlement are excluded from this reference. Refit on the new as-of snapshot. Keep those choices fixed for the reference route; any adaptation must be named and justified. The supplied old selection result, candidate winner, and output tables do not transfer.

An alternative route is required for comparison. Start with the interpretable `weekly_median56` candidate defined in the supplied experiment file, unless an input audit shows it cannot be implemented. It uses the latest 56 days of available demand, grouped by store, item, and weekday, with a documented fallback for an empty group. Any additional method requires a material question, an admissible feature set, and a frozen comparison. A more complex route earns no preference without evidence.

## Acceptance gates and stop conditions

Preflight checks source availability, Python package/runtime needs, writable output domain, and whether an independent reader is actually available. A green preflight is not a result. The first smoke is the probe in `probe.py`: one complete 96-pair future date, one complete historical 96-dimensional residual window, nominal interval fields, integer optimizer output, source timestamps, and original daily constraints. It only demonstrates plumbing and one feasible computed plan under its chosen scenario sample. The current version emits `probe-output-v2/scenarios.csv` and `feature_lineage.csv` so a receiver can recompute the output and inspect the timestamp boundary; the earlier output remains retained in `probe-output/`.

Full acceptance requires a frozen, reproducible 42-day result, every requested comparison and explanation, exact constraint/objective recomputation, source-time integrity, uncertainty caveats, paper-to-result traceability, and independent receiving review. Keep producer self-checks, accepted outputs, and independent verdicts as separate states. If evidence is incomplete, label it pending or blocked at the affected branch; do not lower a gate, invent missing measurements, or convert a design/probe into completed production.

Continue after recoverable tool errors when a changed command or understood correction is supported; keep each failed attempt and its unique receipt. Distinguish runtime recovery, a substantive defect, and a planned research iteration. Stop a route when it repeats the same failure without new information, a real dependency/authority/resource boundary prevents progress, the requested artifact is complete, or the user stops work. Unknown telemetry stays null.
