# R24: Necessity and language-risk review

**Status: proposal/candidate only. No host-level behavior test, independent
new-actor evaluation or release acceptance has been performed here.**

## What was wrong with the preceding proposal

The previous C14 sketch itself used strong language: enumerate *all*
delivery dimensions; use a full receiver manifest; centralize every
repeated figure in a `fact_id` registry; perform a fixed cross-domain
challenge set. Those can be good mechanisms when triggered, but are not
universal duties. Applied blindly they would turn an ordinary small task
into paperwork and create false mandatory failure criteria.

The original C13 already says to derive outcomes, check information flow
and preserve consistency. R22/R23 failures demonstrate execution gaps in
specific cases, not proof that every future task must load more procedure.
This candidate therefore targets *decision triggers* and portability, not
the maximum number of requirements.

## Requirement-strength contract

| Strength | Meaning | Example | What not to infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit mandatory | Direct original user or authorized rule | Both named methods must have their own future results | Reviewer cannot add a third method |
| Necessarily derived | Without it, the requested outcome or reliable claim is impossible | Recompute binding optimizer constraints before claiming feasibility | Full proof of global optimality is not automatic |
| Conditional | Activation tied to a task property or claim | Inspect reviewer channels when claiming blind independence | Not every simple handoff needs a full manifest |
| Optional | Useful but not required for acceptance | More baselines, a richer dashboard, another research source | Missing optional work is not a FAIL |
| Blocked/unverified | Required evidence/capability unavailable | Browser check forbidden/unavailable | Static checks do not become runtime PASS |

A consequential *necessarily derived* duty needs a written causal justification.
Requirements introduced after failures cannot retrospectively change an old
frozen grader. Describe observed failures separately from hypotheses about
why a text change might help.

## Four target mechanisms and anti-overreach guards

1. **Outcome coverage.** Trigger when multiple deliverable instances are
   explicitly or necessarily demanded. Compare required to produced, using
   a compact schema or simple checklist. Skip elaborate matrices for
   a single small result. Source: R23 j1 omitted per-method outputs.
2. **Independent-information hygiene.** Trigger when a new actor's
   *independence* is part of the claim. Inspect the forwarded material,
   including retained messages/commands/summaries where accessible.
   Unknown host visibility means `unverified`, not proof of isolation.
   A detailed packet manifest is an option, not a quota. Source: R23 j4.
3. **Cross-output meaning.** Trigger when the same important values or
   claims appear in multiple consumer formats. Check shared provenance,
   units, operations and displayed renderings. A full fact store is not
   necessary for a short output. Source: R22 h6.
4. **Separated iteration goals.** Routine delivery stops when accepted
   or truthfully blocked. Continued capability exploration requires
   an ongoing authorized research scope, meaningful next hypothesis
   and an actual testable next action. It does not mean endless stages.

## Wording checklist (evaluate semantics, not banned words)

- Must: name a real requirement or necessary evidence and what fails without it.
- When/if: name the activation condition and proportionate action.
- May/prefer: state a reason and tradeoff; never covertly grade it as mandatory.
- Example: mark an illustration as non-normative, without fixed counts.
- Claims: distinguish **designed**, **produced**, **self-checked**,
  **independently accepted**, and **generalized**.
- Failure: preserve original verdict/limit; treat a later fix as informed,
  not retroactive blind success.
- Stop: avoid both a universal two-error cap and an infinite auto-loop.

## Explicit non-goals

No replacement for C13 on main; no alteration of R20/R22/R23 verdicts,
R08 gates or historical budgets; no newly implied backend execution,
provider permission, competition rule, fixed model/agent count, or mandatory
research checklist. The skill remains a portable nine-Markdown method.
