# Week 9 demand and allocation: an offline practice note

## Abstract

This small self-created exercise forecasts weekly demand at three community delivery stations from eight observed weeks and allocates 60 units for week 9. A linear trend fitted separately by station gives forecasts of 28.00, 20.36, and 30.68 units/week for A, B, and C. Rolling one-step validation across five origins per station gives pooled MAE 0.818 units/week for the trend and 1.333 for a last-observation baseline. An integer allocation of (28, 2, 30) minimizes the stated delivery-plus-shortage objective at 264.93 CNY under the provided costs, penalties, caps and stock limit. This is a bounded demonstration, not evidence of contest competitiveness.

## Formulation and method

Observed demand is available only for weeks 1–8. For each station, ordinary least squares fits `y_t = alpha + beta*t + error` on those weeks and extrapolates one week, clipping at zero. Rolling-origin validation uses target weeks 4–8 and fits each prediction only on earlier observations. This respects the no-future requirement. The linear trend is selected as a simple interpretable working method; its lower error on these 15 station-origin predictions is descriptive only, since the sample is tiny and shares the same short series. No calibrated prediction interval is estimated.

For each station `i`, let `x_i` be integer units delivered and `f_i` the week-9 forecast. The objective is `sum_i(c_i*x_i + p_i*max(f_i-x_i,0))`; constraints are integer `0 <= x_i <= cap_i` and `sum_i x_i <= 60`. A SciPy MILP uses shortage variables and requests zero relative MIP gap. A separate exhaustive check evaluates all 24,076 feasible integer allocations and returns the same optimum, providing exact finite-instance optimality evidence for these forecast values and supplied parameters.

## Results and interpretation

The selected forecasts are A 28.00, B 20.36, C 30.68 units/week. The plan delivers A 28, B 2, C 30 units (60 total), below respective caps 30, 25, 35. Shortage against forecast is 0, 18.36, and 0.68 units. Delivery cost is 56 + 2 + 90 = 148 CNY; shortage penalty is approximately 110.14 + 6.79 CNY; total objective is 264.93 CNY. The binding stock limit directs scarce units toward larger penalty-minus-delivery-cost tradeoffs, subject to the demand-based shortage objective.

## Limits

The data comprise only eight weeks and three stations, with no external context, seasonality assessment, uncertainty calibration, or independent acceptance. The data do not establish empirical superiority outside the selected rolling origins. The objective treats forecast demand as the shortage reference and uses exact supplied linear costs/penalties; different values can change the plan. This practice exercise involved no online research: no literature, public implementation, current contest rules, or organizer claims were checked. No award, rank, causal effect, real-world service result, or general Level-6 capability claim follows.
