# Offline practice report: weekly demand allocation

## Questions and data

This is a small self-created offline exercise. Weeks 1–8 provide demand for stations A, B and C in units/week. The task forecasts week 9, then allocates at most 60 integer units under caps A=30, B=25, C=35. Delivery costs are 2, 1 and 3 CNY/unit; unmet forecast penalties are 8, 6 and 10 CNY/unit. There is no week-9 observed demand, so the forecast cannot be checked against a future outcome here.

The source has eight chronological rows, all 24 station-week cells present, no duplicate weeks, and numeric nonnegative values from 15 to 30. Units are consistent within the supplied schema. No future outcome or external dataset was used.

## Forecast method and validation

I compared last-value persistence against a least-squares linear trend. Validation used expanding-origin one-step forecasts for weeks 4–8: every prediction used only weeks preceding its target. The pooled MAE across the 15 station-fold observations was 1.3333 units/week for persistence and 0.8181 for the linear trend. Under the declared rule of lower pooled MAE (ties favor persistence), the selected method was the linear trend. Refit on weeks 1–8, it forecasts week 9 at A=28.000, B=20.357, C=30.679 units/week. The lower historical MAE is descriptive for this tiny series and does not establish future or general superiority.

## Integer optimization

For indivisible deliveries, I rounded each forecast upward to demand units: A=28, B=21, C=31. The objective is

\[\min_x \sum_i c_i x_i + p_i\max(d_i-x_i,0),\quad x_i\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0},\ x_i\le u_i,\ \sum_i x_i\le60.\]

The resulting plan is A=28, B=1, C=31 (60 units total). It respects every station cap. Its recomputed objective is A: 2×28=56 CNY; B: 1×1 + 6×20=121 CNY; C: 3×31=93 CNY; total 270 CNY.

An exact optimality argument follows from separability: each unit through rounded demand reduces the objective by penalty minus delivery cost, respectively 6, 5 and 7 CNY. There are 80 such positive-value units; choosing the 60 highest gains gives C=31, A=28, B=1. The last selected and best omitted positive gain are both 5 CNY, so ties do not change optimality. Units beyond rounded demand increase cost without reducing shortage. This establishes global optimality for the stated forecasts, coefficients and constraints, not for unknown true demand.

## Conclusion and limits

The historical trend rule performed better than persistence on the selected five expanding-origin folds, so I used it for the week-9 estimate. Under the supplied penalty/cost tradeoff and 60-unit stock, the exact plan prioritizes station C, then A, and leaves station B mostly unmet. The plan is sensitive to the supplied penalties, which are treated as given rather than empirically estimated. The small synthetic series cannot support claims about actual community demand, MathorCup outcomes, competition rank, awards or broad model performance. No external research or current organizer rules were consulted because this is an offline practice task.
