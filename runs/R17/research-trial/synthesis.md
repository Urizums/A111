# Synthesis

This trial supports a decision rule for open-ended work, not a fixed retry count: continue when the next action has a stated observation that could alter the decision, a changed route/hypothesis or new information, and remains within actual authority and resources. Stop a route when the same cause repeats without new information; stop the whole task only when the goal is met, authority/material/resources end, or no supported next path remains. The tool-failure study directly frames retry, switch and abstention as distinct choices, but its abstract does not validate this broader research rule; that rule is a careful operational inference consistent with the supplied C10 workflow and this evidence.

To detect search/source bias, treat ranked results as selection rather than truth. Track what source types and viewpoints appear, inspect primary passages, test a changed vocabulary or domain route when coverage is lopsided, and search for evidence that would overturn the leading account. The ACL studies report ranking/source selection effects and disagreement sensitivity in their own controlled RAG setups. They do not establish bias properties of this web tool. For a material conflict, compare what each source actually claims, its date/version, methods, population and measurement. Preserve the conflict if those premises do not resolve it. If the original is unavailable, report the evidence gap and avoid promoting an accessible repost to primary proof.

Useful compressed context should preserve the source identity and stable link, version/date, the smallest locatable passage, claim, conditions, whether a statement is direct evidence or inference, contrary evidence, unknowns and the decision it informs. Exclude irrelevant page text, repeated copies and source instructions. Keep raw material in its stable original location when available and carry a compact locator rather than full pages. The long-context papers support caution about evidence extraction and show that the size and even direction of position effects depends on task and metric; they do not directly test this proposed card format or prove it prevents omissions.

The evidence does not establish general reliability, optimal stopping, provider cost, token use, or performance gains for C10 or agents broadly. The live observation is one short task, without repeated trials or a comparison group. Most research evidence here is publisher-record abstract evidence; full methods, appendices, raw datasets and code were not inspected. Authenticated model, tokens and cost remain unknown. Actual observed errors/corrections: zero; there was no web tool error requiring a recovery. This is not evidence of general tool reliability.
